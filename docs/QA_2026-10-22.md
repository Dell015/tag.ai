# QA pass, 2026-10-22

How it was tested: a production build in headless Chromium at phone size (390 x 844), a fresh first-run onboarding, all 16 routes, a full drinking game for 40 cards (tools, undo, pause menu), a desktop view and an offline reload. Plus `npm audit`, lint, 242 unit tests and a contrast check. **Not tested: a real phone, the recap screen in this pass, two phones at once (there is no database yet).**

## What works

- Onboarding: intro, age gate, nickname and colour, 10 welcome coins, lands on home.
- Every screen loads with no crash and no console errors: Home, Shop, Me, Wallet, Settings, Saved, Suggest, Leaderboard, Invite, Trashed, Topic deck, Help, Terms, Privacy, Join. The 404 page shows "Wrong turn".
- A full game: add friends, buy cards, topics, rules, tap-to-agree, play 40 cards with dice, coin, timer cards animating on their own screen, heat reaching "Anything goes", pause menu with heat, mood, focus, pot, players.
- Unit tests: 242 pass. Lint and type-check are clean.

## Must fix before telling friends

1. **Security: Next.js 14.2.35 has a critical advisory** (`npm audit`; fix is a major upgrade to Next 16). Also three high ones inside its dependencies. Upgrade, then re-run tests, lint and build.
2. **Legal pages are placeholders** ("This is placeholder text"). Replace with plain honest text before anyone outside your circle uses it.
3. **Test on real phones**: an iPhone in Safari, a mid-range Android, and a cheap Android. Hold a real 30 minute game.
4. **Recap screen**: re-test after a full game (it worked earlier, but this pass did not reach it).
5. **Feedback channel**: a "Send feedback" link so testers can report problems.

## Should fix soon

- **Offline**: the app shows an offline banner but does not cache pages; reloading `/shop` offline fails. Needs a service worker.
- **Contrast**: white text on the blue (4.1:1), purple (4.0:1) and red (4.1:1) card colours is just under the 4.5:1 target for small text. Large card text is fine. Darken those three shades a little or use dark text on them.
- **Small tap targets** (under 44px): Undo button (40px), move and remove buttons in the pause menu (41 x 45), and the settings switches (visually 53 x 30, the whole row is tappable).
- **Coins are editable in the browser** (anyone can change their balance in the developer tools). Expected until the database exists; do not sell coins before it is fixed.
- **Shortest game costs 3 coins** (the pot minimum), which is about 30 cards of a cheap deck, so the 10 welcome coins cover about three games. Decide if that is the generosity you want.
