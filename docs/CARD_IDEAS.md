# Card ideas notes

Purpose: collect ideas from the internet and from friends so Claude can learn what makes a good card and what to avoid. **Research notes only. Nothing here goes into the app as written.**

## Rules for collecting
1. Read threads by hand (for example r/AskPH). No scraping, no scripts, no bulk downloads. Reddit's terms forbid it and posts are people's personal data.
2. Paste short notes, not whole threads: the question, roughly how many replies it got, and why it worked.
3. Strip usernames, names, schools, workplaces and any personal story.
4. Never copy a post or comment into a card. Write a new card in your own words, in the style of `docs/CARD_GUIDE.md`.
5. Every written card goes through the blocked-word and duplicate checks, then admin approval.

## What to note per idea
`theme | shape (truth, dare, tool, vote) | heat 1 to 5 | why it worked | what would make it too much`
