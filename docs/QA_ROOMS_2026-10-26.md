# QA: online rooms (2026-10-26)

Needs two accounts on two devices (call them **Host** and **Friend**). A third device or a guest name is optional.
Before you start: give both accounts at least 30 coins (Admin, Coins). Keep a note of every balance, so you can check the coins.
Mark each line Pass, Fail, or Odd, and write what you saw for anything that is not Pass.

## A. Accounts and friends
1. Friend creates a new account (email and password, or Google). Time it. Target: under 60 seconds. __
2. Host, Me, Friends: add Friend by player name. Friend sees a request on Me, Friends and taps Accept. Both lists show each other. __
3. Add a name that does not exist: a clear message, no crash. __
4. Add yourself: a clear message. __
5. Send a request twice: a clear message. __
6. Online dot: Friend has the app open, Host's list shows a green dot within about a minute. Friend closes the app, the dot goes grey after about 2 minutes. __
7. Remove a friend, add them again. __

## B. Room and invite
1. Host starts a game, adds a guest by name, taps Invite friends. A code and QR appear. __
2. Friend opens the QR link (or Join a room and types the code). Friend lands in the lobby and appears in the player list on both phones within a few seconds. __
3. A person with no account opens the QR link: sends them to create an account, then straight into the room. __
4. Host taps Invite a friend, Invite next to Friend's name. Friend gets an inbox message with a Join the room button. It works. Tapping Invite again right away says already invited. __
5. Wrong code on Join: a clear message. Eight wrong codes in a minute: a lock message. __
6. Friend leaves the room. Host sees them go. Friend joins again. __
7. Host removes Friend from the list. Friend's phone says the room is closed or they were removed. __

## C. Setup and the shared pot
1. Host picks two decks and the pot number. Friend sees the bar and the amount needed. __
2. Friend taps +5. Both phones show the bar and Friend's name under the bar. Friend's wallet drops by 5 (check Me, Wallet). __
3. Host adds the rest. Bar is full and the host's Start button turns on. __
4. Host tries to lower the pot below what is paid: refused with a message. __
5. Friend leaves before the start: Friend's coins come back (wallet and history show a refund). __
6. Seat circle: tap names in order. Numbers appear. Tap a seated name to take it off. Shuffle for me works. Start over works. __
7. Everyone taps their own name to agree (each on their own phone). The host cannot start until all agree. __
8. House rules step: Which phone runs the game? Pick Friend. __

## D. Playing and watching
1. Host taps Start. Friend's phone shows Continue the game (or the live view if Host chose their own phone). __
2. Game phone reveals a card. The other phone shows the card text within a few seconds. A face-down card shows no text on the watching phone. __
3. Next card, skip, trash, save, undo: the watching phone follows (current player, card count, heat, next up). __
4. Rule card: the watching phone shows Rule on. __
5. Watching phone adds coins to the pot (+5). Game phone shows a toast, pot goes up. Wallet drops. __
6. Pot empties during play: the game pauses with Pot is empty. Friend adds coins from the watching phone. The game resumes by itself. __

## E. Pass and take over
1. Pause, Pass the game phone, pick Friend. Friend's phone shows Continue the game and continues on the same card. Host's phone now watches. __
2. Pass back. __
3. Take over: on the game phone, turn the data off (or close the app) for 60 seconds. On the other phone, Run the game on this phone appears after about 45 seconds. Tap it. The game continues from the last saved card. __
4. The old phone comes back online. It says another phone is running the game and goes to the lobby. It does not move the game. __
5. A stranger or a phone not in the room cannot reach the game (try the room code in a private window with no account). __

## F. Ending
1. Last card: the recap shows on the game phone, the other phone shows Game over. __
2. Keep going: change the number of cards, tap Keep going. If the pot is short, Add coins to the pot works and then the cards are added. __
3. Play again (game phone): room returns to the lobby, the pot is empty, nobody has agreed. Both phones can start again. __
4. Host taps End the game for everyone while only watching. __
5. Coins: after a game, no coins were refunded (the rules say so on screen). Check wallet history for both accounts against your notes. __

## G. Closing and bad connections
1. Close the room from the lobby as host: Friend's coins come back, Friend's phone says the room is closed. __
2. Leave a room alone for 60 minutes with no phone open: inbox message Your room closed arrives on both accounts. In a lobby, coins are back. __
3. Turn Wi-Fi off on Friend's phone for 20 seconds in the lobby, then on: the room catches up. __
4. Lock the Friend's phone for a minute and open it: the room catches up. __

## H. One phone only (the old way must still work)
1. No Invite: add two guests by name, pick decks, pay from the host wallet, play. Pot refunded at the end if cards are left. __
2. 10 more cards at the recap charges the host's wallet. __

## I. Look and feel (phones)
1. Nothing wider than the screen. The tab bar stays centred. __
2. The seat circle, the pot bar, the live view and the friends list read well at 360 px wide. __
3. Taps are easy to hit one-handed. __

## Known gaps (not bugs for this round)
- The app checks for changes every 3 seconds, so expect a short delay.
- Cards are built on the phone that starts the game.
- The room-closing email is not sent yet (inbox message only).
- Only the host can invite friends from the lobby. No friend code yet.
